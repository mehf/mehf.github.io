Conjugation basics: Lithuanian verbs change by person and tense. Persons: aš (I), tu (you), jis/ji (he/she), mes (we), jūs (you pl./formal), jie/jos (they m./f.). Handy pattern: the 3rd person is the same singular and plural, and the future is usually infinitive-stem + -siu/-si/-s.
